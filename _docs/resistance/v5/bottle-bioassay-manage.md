View and manage saved bottle bioassay records.

## Filtering Bioassays

Use the filter above the table to choose which bottle bioassays are displayed.

- **Date Range**: Display bottle bioassays conducted within a selected date range.
- **Recent**: Display the most recently updated bottle bioassays.

Your filter selection and settings are remembered for future visits.

---

## Bottle Bioassay Management Table

The table lists bottle bioassay records for your current agency, including test names, dates, testers, species, mosquito sources, and ingredients.

### Table Features

- **Filtering**: Use the search box to filter the displayed records by ID, test name, date, tester, species, source type, sources, or ingredients.
- **Pagination**: Navigate between pages when more records are available than can be displayed at once.

### Action Buttons

![Pencil icon](/assets/images/docs/action-button-edit.png): Open the selected bottle bioassay to view its details or make changes.

![Red trash can icon](/assets/images/docs/action-button-delete.png): Delete bottle bioassay.

### Adding a Bottle Bioassay

Click **Add New Bottle Bioassay** to open the [New Bottle Bioassay]({{ site.baseurl }}/docs/resistance/v5/bottle-bioassay) form.
